  A29=1. vs. business SMS platforms (your real peers): Your ~$0.038 list sits in the low-middle of the $0.018–$0.078 band. Fairly/competitively priced; no longer underpriced at list.
  A30=2. At high volume your ~$0.0165/credit still undercuts the 50K tiers (~$0.018–$0.019), and margin there now holds ~30% (up from ~24% at the old 3.05 markup). Better balance of price leadership and retained margin.
  A31=3. vs. CPaaS (Twilio/Telnyx/Plivo): Your list (~$0.030/segment) is still ~3–4x their all-in wholesale rate ($0.008–$0.015/seg). That gap is the managed-application markup — expected, and the same premium SimpleTexting/SlickText charge over the same pipe.
  A32=4. Structure matches the market: credit model + MMS = 3 credits mirrors SimpleTexting/SlickText exactly, so buyers can compare apples-to-apples. Your credit = 1.3 segments is slightly more generous than their 1-segment credit.
  A33=5. Nonprofit angle: SimpleTexting gives 501(c)(3)s 15% off. If you sell to schools/nonprofits, consider matching a nonprofit discount so your effective price stays below theirs.
